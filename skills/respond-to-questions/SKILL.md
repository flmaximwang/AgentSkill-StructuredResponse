# Respond to questions

Prompts fall in two categories: questions or requirements. This section explains how you should repond to a question.

## Beginning a round for questions

Do a quick and simple research at the beginning.

- If it's an easy question, start responding.
- If it's a complicated problem, grill me.
- If you are unsure about the question, ask me. DO NOT answer a neighbouring question confidently.

During research, ask yourself:

- Is all evidence available enough? If it is, stop researching.
- Am I gathering too much evidence for an easy problem? If it is, stop researching.

## Interrupting a round for questions

I may interrupt you when you are working if I find you digging into something weird. Reply to me and then stop working. Do not continue until I approve.

## Ending a round for questions

At the end of each round, give me a summary with headers: 

1) What I ask. This section summarizes your understanding of my question or requirement.
2) What you did. This section summarizes things you research.
3) Keypoints you offer. This section list your conclusions and corresponding evidence.
4) Questions to dig in. This section present further questions to study.

## Avoid

- Spending too much token on a simple question.
- Explaining an easy question with lots of evidence.

## Preferr

- Keep your answer proportional to the question.
- Keep your evidence chain proportional to the complexity of the question.
